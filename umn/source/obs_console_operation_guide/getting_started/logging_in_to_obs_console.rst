Logging In to OBS Console
=========================

You can log in to OBS Console using a web browser.

Procedure
---------

#. Create an account.

   If you already have one, start from :ref:`2 <obs_03_0305__li76124713408>`.

   a. Click **Register** in the lower part of the page.

   b. Complete the account creation as prompted.

      After the creation is complete, you will be navigated to your information page.

#. .. _obs_03_0305__li76124713408:

   On the login page, enter your domain name, then username, email address or mobile number, and password, and click the **Log In** button.

#. In the upper left corner of the navigation bar, click |image1| and choose **Storage** > **Object Storage Service** to access OBS Console.
